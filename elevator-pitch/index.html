I’m Sai Keerthan Palavarapu, a Senior Software Engineer with 5+ years of experience building backend systems and cloud infrastructure. I work in Go, Java, and Python across Kubernetes, AWS, and Azure.

My experience spans cloud and AI infrastructure at Microsoft, e-commerce and order orchestration at Citymall, and financial analytics at QuantumStreet AI. My work includes MCP services, event-driven microservices, streaming pipelines, and observability.

Selected results include 40% lower product catalog P99 API latency at peak loads of 2K+ requests/second, reducing data availability lag from 12 hours to under eight minutes, and cutting infrastructure costs by 60% across 450+ ETL workflows processing over 1TB/day.

My projects include a C++ distributed filesystem prototype using gRPC and FUSE, and a hybrid RAG Knowledge Assistant using ChromaDB, BM25, OpenAI embeddings, Cohere reranking, and LangChain. I’ve also submitted public pull requests to RocksDB and the Azure Developer CLI.

I hold an MS in Computer Science from Syracuse University. I’m based in Bellevue, WA, and interested in backend, cloud infrastructure, and distributed systems engineering opportunities.
